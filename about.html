Our story

A kitchen dream,
shared with Lahore.

Sweet Crumbs began with one well-loved mixing bowl, a family recipe book and a simple idea: make the kind of bakes that bring people together. Today, every loaf and layer cake still begins with that same care.

We make in small batches, choose ingredients thoughtfully and give every recipe the time it deserves. Because we’re not just baking for a display case — we’re baking for your table, your celebrations and your everyday little joys.

With love, from our kitchen.
